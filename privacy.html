Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026. Applies to ECLIPSE for macOS.

In short: ECLIPSE collects no personal data. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code, and it sends nothing to the developer. There is no sign-up.

Stored on your Mac

Your player profile and its progress (campaign, decks, achievements, mastery, quests, cosmetic rewards, statistics), your settings, the match in progress, replays, automatic profile backups and a technical journal (Logs/) used only to build a problem report. They live in the game's folder under Library/Application Support. Settings → Data shows the folder, exports or imports your progress and restores backups.

Your iCloud (optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game keeps one profile's progress in your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) so it reaches your other Macs. That data belongs to your Apple Account: only you can read it — the developer cannot see or access it. Settings, replays and the match in progress are not uploaded.

Settings → Account → iCloud shows the sync and turns it off. Apple processes iCloud data under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

In the Mac App Store version, Apple's Game Center may recognise you automatically and give the game your Game Center display name and a game-specific player identifier, which the game uses to name your profile. Online play is optional: during an online match the other player's Mac receives your Game Center display name, your deck and your moves. Wins and the daily-challenge score are posted to Game Center leaderboards and achievements to Game Center. Apple handles your Game Center identity under its own privacy policy; you can sign out under System Settings → Game Center.

Crash reports and problem reports

If you share analytics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple may give the game anonymous crash and hang diagnostics (MetricKit). The game keeps them in Logs/ on your Mac.

“Report a problem” shows the full report before anything is written: the game and macOS versions, your Mac model, settings, the technical journal and (if you leave it on) the current match's moves. It contains no account name. Nothing is sent automatically — you decide whether to share the saved file.

Local network

“Host on this Mac” accepts connections from your local network only, and only while it is switched on.

Children

ECLIPSE has no chat, no purchases and no user-generated content, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Settings → Account → Delete account erases that profile and everything it saved on your Mac. To remove the iCloud copy, turn off iCloud sync for the profile first, or delete the game's data under System Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage. Uninstalling the game removes it from your Mac.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: see Support.
