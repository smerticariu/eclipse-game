Support

Something not working, or a question about the game? Here is the fastest way to get help.

Contact

Open a request on the ECLIPSE support tracker (a free GitHub account is needed). Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened, and include your game version from Settings → Data → About.

Requests on the tracker are public: do not post personal information there. Answers usually come within a few days.

Report a problem

In the game: Settings → Data → Report a problem… (or Esc → Report a problem during a match). The game shows the whole report before saving it — the game and macOS versions, your Mac model, settings and a technical journal; it contains no account name. Attach the saved file to your support request.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. The game creates your player profile on first launch. In the Mac App Store version, Game Center recognises you automatically and names your profile.

How do I get my progress on another Mac?

Sign in to the same iCloud account on both Macs. Your campaign, decks, achievements, mastery and rewards sync on their own; you can see the state under Settings → Account → iCloud. If you played on two Macs without internet, both sets of progress are merged and nothing is lost. Settings, replays and a match in progress stay on each Mac.

Without iCloud, use Settings → Data → Export progress… and import the file on the other Mac.

I lost progress — can I get it back?

The game backs up your profile automatically (the last five versions). Settings → Data → Backups restores one; your current profile is backed up first.

How does online play work?

Online matches go through Game Center: sign in under System Settings → Game Center, then choose Quick match or invite a friend. Both players need the same version of ECLIPSE. A player who leaves a match, or misses three turns in a row, forfeits it. On one network you can also play with “Host on this Mac”.

Is there anything to buy inside the game?

No. All 240 cards and 24 heroes are included from the first launch. Rewards are cosmetic only — titles, card backs, battlefields and foil frames.

Which Macs and languages are supported?

Macs with Apple silicon running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. The game is in English, Romanian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Polish (Settings → Language).

Accessibility

Every action works from the keyboard (Settings → Controls lists the keys). VoiceOver reads every card; Reduce Motion, high contrast and a colour-blind palette are under Settings → Accessibility, interface scaling for large displays under Settings → Display. Cinematics have subtitles and can be skipped or turned off.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Account → Delete account erases that profile from your Mac. To remove the copy in your iCloud, turn off “Keep this account's progress in iCloud” first, or delete the game's data under System Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage. See the privacy policy.
