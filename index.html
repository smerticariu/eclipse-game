Tactical card battles

Three lanes. Real positioning.

Every unit stands in the Front or the Rear of one of three lanes. Guard, Ranged and Flank turn placement into strategy, and Command lets you move your army mid-battle. Fair and deterministic: no packs, no paid power — every card is yours from the first launch.

A story worth finishing

The sun has stopped. You are the Herald.

Guided by Kaleb, you carry the last Spark of the Heart of Dawn through eight realms toward the Eclipser — and learn what the old rite really costs. Painted cinematics, dialogue before and after every mission, bosses who talk back, a Chronicle that fills in as you play, and an ending you choose.

Ways to play

Something for every evening

Campaign

24 missions across eight chapters, with boss battles, 72 stars to earn and two endings.

Expedition

A nine-floor roguelike descent: new cards and relics after each win, three lives.

Puzzles

12 hand-made “win this turn” puzzles, each one verified solvable.

Daily challenge

The same seeded battle for everyone, every day, with a score to beat.

Skirmish

An AI with three levels of difficulty and a personality for every faction.

Online

Made for the Mac

Native, private, yours

Built in Swift for Apple silicon. Keyboard play, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, high contrast, a colour-blind palette and interface scaling for large displays, in eight languages. No sign-up: your progress follows you to your other Macs through your own iCloud, and the game sends nothing to us.
